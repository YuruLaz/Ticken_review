# Changing Your Email Address or Profile Details

You can update your name, email address, phone number, and saved addresses from your ShopNest account at any time.

## Change your email address

1. Go to **Account > Profile** (in the app: **Profile** tab > **Personal info**).
2. Select **Edit** next to your email address.
3. Enter the new email address and your current password.
4. Open the verification email sent to the new address and select the link.

The verification link is valid for **24 hours**. Your old email address stays active until you verify the new one. Once the change is complete, we send a notice to your old address. If you didn't make the change, contact support immediately.

You can change your email address once every **7 days**. Each email address can be linked to only one ShopNest account.

## Change your name or phone number

Edit your name or phone number in **Account > Profile** and select **Save**. Changes apply right away. A new phone number must be verified with a 6-digit code sent by SMS.

## Manage saved addresses

You can save up to **10 shipping addresses** in **Account > Addresses**. Set one as your default to have it selected automatically at checkout.

## Changes to existing orders

Updating your profile does not change orders you have already placed. To change the shipping address on an existing order, open it in **Account > Orders** and select **Change address**. This is only possible until the order ships.

## Communication preferences

To choose which marketing emails you receive, go to **Account > Notifications**. Order and security emails are always sent and cannot be turned off.
