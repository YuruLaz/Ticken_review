# Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your ShopNest account and personal data at any time. Please read this before you start, because deletion cannot be undone once it is complete.

## Before you delete

- You cannot delete your account while you have an order that has not been delivered, or an open return or refund. Wait until these are finished.
- Download any invoices you need. You lose access to them once the account is deleted.
- Cancel any active SplitPay installment plans by paying the remaining balance.

## How to delete your account

1. Go to **Account > Privacy > Delete account** (in the app: **Profile** tab > **Settings** > **Delete account**).
2. Choose a reason (optional).
3. Enter your password and select **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm using the link we send to your email address.

## What happens next

- Your account is deactivated immediately. You are signed out on all devices.
- Your account is **permanently deleted after 30 days**. During those 30 days, you can cancel the deletion by simply signing in again.
- After 30 days, your profile, saved addresses, saved payment methods, wish lists, and reviews are erased.

## What we keep

By law, we keep records of your purchases, such as invoices and payment records, for **7 years** for tax and accounting purposes. These records are not used for marketing.

## Unsubscribing instead

If you just want fewer emails, you don't need to delete your account. Go to **Account > Notifications** to turn off marketing emails, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email.

## Creating a new account later

You can create a new account with the same email address once the 30-day period has ended. Your old order history will not be restored.
