# Resetting Your Password

If you forgot your ShopNest password, you can reset it yourself in a few minutes.

## How to reset your password

1. Go to the sign-in page on the website or app and select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your ShopNest account.
3. Open the email from ShopNest with the subject "Reset your password" and select the reset link.
4. Enter a new password and confirm it.

## Things to know

- Reset links expire after **1 hour** and can only be used once. If yours has expired, request a new one.
- You can request up to **5 reset emails per hour**. After that, wait an hour before trying again.
- Only the most recent link works. Requesting a new link cancels older ones.
- After you reset your password, you are signed out on all other devices.

## Password requirements

Your new password must:

- Be at least **10 characters** long
- Contain at least one letter and one number
- Not be the same as any of your last 3 passwords

## I didn't get the reset email

- Wait 5 minutes, then check your spam, junk, and promotions folders.
- Make sure you typed the email address you used to create the account. For security, we show the same message whether or not an account exists for that address.
- Add no-reply@shopnest.com to your contacts and request a new link.

If you signed up with Apple or Google, you don't have a ShopNest password. Use the **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google** button instead.

## Changing a password you know

If you just want a new password, go to **Account > Security > Change password**. You will need to enter your current password.
