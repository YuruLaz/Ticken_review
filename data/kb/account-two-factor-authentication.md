# Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in, so no one can access your account with just your password.

## Turn on 2FA

1. Go to **Account > Security > Two-factor authentication**.
2. Choose a method:
   - **Authenticator app** (recommended), such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy. Scan the QR code with the app.
   - **SMS**, sent to a verified mobile phone number.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
4. Save your **10 backup codes** somewhere safe.

## Signing in with 2FA

After entering your password, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app or text message.

- Authenticator app codes change every **30 seconds**.
- SMS codes are valid for **10 minutes**.
- Select **Trust this device** to skip the code on that device for **30 days**.

## Backup codes

Each backup code works only once. Use one if you lose your phone or can't receive codes. You can generate a new set of 10 codes in **Account > Security** at any time. Doing so cancels all old codes.

## Lost access to your 2FA method

If you have no access to your codes or backup codes, contact support. For your safety, we verify your identity using your order history and the payment method on file. Recovery takes up to **3 business days**, and 2FA is turned off once it is complete.

## Turning off 2FA

Go to **Account > Security > Two-factor authentication** and select **Turn off**. You need to enter your password and a current code. We email you whenever 2FA is turned on or off.

## Sign in with Apple or Google

If you sign in with Apple or Google, 2FA is managed by that account, not by ShopNest.
