# Double Charges and Failed Payments

Most unexpected charges and failed payments resolve on their own. Here is what to check.

## I was charged twice

In most cases, one of the charges is a **pending authorization**, not a real charge. This happens when a payment is retried, for example after a timeout or when you pressed "Place order" twice.

- Pending authorizations disappear automatically within **3-5 business days**. They never turn into a real charge.
- Check **Account > Orders**. If you see only one order, you will only be charged once.
- If an order ships in multiple packages, you will see one charge per package. These add up to your order total.

If both charges are still showing as completed after 5 business days, contact support with your order number and a screenshot of your statement. We refund confirmed duplicate charges within **1 business day** of verifying them.

## My payment failed

Common reasons:

- The billing address does not match the address on file with your bank.
- The card has expired or has insufficient funds.
- Your bank blocked the payment as suspicious. Approve it in your banking app or call your bank.
- The CVV or expiry date was entered incorrectly.

## What happens to my order

If a payment fails at checkout, no order is created. If a payment fails when we try to capture it at shipping, your order is put **on hold for 48 hours** and we email you a link to update your payment method. If the payment is not updated within 48 hours, the order is cancelled automatically and any authorization is released.

After **3 failed attempts in 24 hours**, checkout is paused for that card for 24 hours for security reasons. You can use a different payment method right away.
