# Downloading Invoices

Every ShopNest order comes with a PDF invoice you can download from your account at any time. Invoices are useful for expense reports, warranty claims, and tax records.

## On the website

1. Sign in and go to **Account > Orders**.
2. Find the order and click **View details**.
3. Click **Download invoice (PDF)**.

## In the mobile app

1. Tap the **Profile** tab, then **Orders**.
2. Open the order.
3. Tap **Invoice**, then **Share** or **Save to Files**.

## When the invoice becomes available

The invoice is created when your order ships, and it appears in your account **within 24 hours of shipping**. Before that, you can download an order confirmation, but it is not an invoice. If an order ships in several packages, you get one invoice per package.

## What the invoice includes

- Invoice number and order number
- Billing and shipping address
- Item names, serial numbers (for phones and laptops), prices, and quantities
- Shipping cost, sales tax, and any discounts
- Payment method (card type and last four digits)

## Changing the name or address on an invoice

You can edit the billing name and address on an invoice once, within **30 days** of it being issued. Open the order, click **Edit billing details**, then download the updated invoice. After 30 days, contact support to request a change.

## How long invoices are kept

Invoices stay available in your account for **7 years**. Deleting your account removes your access to them, so download any you need first.
