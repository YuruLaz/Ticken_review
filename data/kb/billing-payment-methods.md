# Accepted Payment Methods

ShopNest accepts the following payment methods on the website and in the iOS and Android apps. All prices are charged in US dollars (USD).

## Cards

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover

Debit cards with a Visa or Mastercard logo are accepted the same way as credit cards.

## Digital wallets

- PayPal
- Apple Pay (Safari and the iOS app)
- Google Pay (Chrome and the Android app)

## Pay in installments with SplitPay

SplitPay lets you split an order into **4 interest-free payments**, charged every 2 weeks. The first payment is charged when you place the order.

- Available for orders between **$100 and $2,000**.
- Approval is decided instantly at checkout and does not affect your credit score.
- A $7 late fee applies if a scheduled payment fails and is not paid within 3 days.

## Saving a payment method

You can save up to 5 cards in **Account > Payment Methods**. Card details are stored by our payment processor, not by ShopNest. We only see the card type and last four digits.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place an order, and the charge is captured when the order ships. If an order ships in multiple packages, you may see a separate charge for each package. The total always matches your order confirmation.

## Not accepted

We do not accept cash on delivery, personal checks, or bank wire transfers for online orders.
