# Refund Policy

ShopNest refunds approved returns and cancelled orders back to the original payment method. This article explains when you get your money back and how much.

## When refunds are issued

- **Cancelled orders:** If you cancel before your order ships, the refund is issued immediately.
- **Returned items:** Our returns team inspects each item within 2 business days of it arriving at our warehouse. Once approved, the refund is issued the same day.
- After a refund is issued, it takes **5-7 business days** to appear on your statement, depending on your bank. PayPal refunds usually appear within 1-2 business days.

## How much is refunded

- Unopened items returned within 30 days of delivery receive a full refund of the item price.
- Opened phones and laptops returned within 30 days of delivery are subject to a **15% restocking fee**. Opened accessories have no restocking fee.
- Original shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged, defective, or was the wrong item.
- Items returned more than 30 days after delivery are not eligible for a refund. Defective items may still be covered by our warranty.

## Partial refunds

If an item comes back with missing parts (chargers, cables, manuals) or with damage that was not present at delivery, we may issue a partial refund. We will email you an explanation and photos before the refund is issued.

## Checking refund status

Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order, and look for the refund status. You will also get an email when your refund is issued. If 7 business days have passed since that email and you still don't see the money, contact your bank first, then our support team with your order number.
