# Contact Options and Support Hours

Our customer support team is happy to help with orders, returns, payments, and product questions. Here is how to reach us.

## Live chat

Chat with an agent from the **Help** page on shopnest.com or from **Profile > Help** in the app.

- Available during phone support hours (below).
- Average wait time: under **3 minutes**.
- Outside these hours, our virtual assistant answers common questions 24/7 and can pass your question to an agent.

## Phone

Call **1-800-555-0147** (toll-free in the US).

- Monday to Friday: **8:00 AM - 8:00 PM ET**
- Saturday and Sunday: **9:00 AM - 5:00 PM ET**
- Closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

Customers outside the US can use live chat or email.

## Email

Write to **support@shopnest.com**. We reply within **24 hours**, 7 days a week. Replies may take up to 48 hours during the holiday season (November 15 to December 31).

## Before you contact us

Have these details ready so we can help you faster:

- Your order number (it starts with **SN-** followed by 8 digits, for example SN-20481937)
- The email address on your account
- Photos or screenshots, if your question is about a damaged item or an app error

For your security, our agents will never ask for your full card number or password.

## Social media

We answer general questions on X and Instagram at **@ShopNest**. For questions about a specific order, please use chat, phone, or email, so we can keep your personal details private.

## Feedback and complaints

If you are not happy with how your issue was handled, ask the agent to escalate it to a supervisor. Supervisors respond within **2 business days**.
