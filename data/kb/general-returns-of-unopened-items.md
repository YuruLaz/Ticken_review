# Returning Unopened Items

Changed your mind? You can return any unopened item to ShopNest for a full refund within **30 days of delivery**, with free return shipping.

## What counts as unopened

- The original factory seal or shrink wrap is intact.
- The item is in its original packaging with all labels.
- The box has not been damaged or written on.


## How to start a return

1. Go to **Account > Orders** (in the app: **Profile** tab > **Orders**).
2. Select the order and choose **Return items**.
3. Pick the items and choose the reason **Unopened / no longer needed**.
4. Choose how to send it back:
   - **Print a free prepaid label** and drop the package at any UPS location.
   - **Show a QR code** at a UPS Store, with no printing or box needed.
   - **Drop it off** at any ShopNest store.

Return labels are valid for **14 days** after you create them. Ship the item within that time.

## Packing your return

Put the unopened product box inside a shipping box. Do not put labels or tape directly on the product box, or it may no longer count as unopened.

## Your refund

We inspect returns within **2 business days** of receiving them. Once approved, you get a full refund of the item price to your original payment method, and it appears within **5-7 business days**. You will receive an email at each step.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Items marked **Final sale** on the product page
- Software, digital downloads, and activated SIM cards
- Items returned more than 30 days after delivery
