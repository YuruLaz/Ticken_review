# Store Locations and Pickup Points

Besides home delivery, you can pick up your ShopNest order for free at one of our stores or at a partner pickup point.

## ShopNest stores

We have 3 stores in the US:

- **New York:** 245 Atlantic Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11201
- **Chicago:** 880 North Michigan Avenue, Chicago, IL 60611
- **Austin:** 1100 South Congress Avenue, Austin, TX 78704

Store hours: **Monday to Saturday 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM** (local time). Stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

At our stores you can shop, pick up orders, drop off returns, and get help setting up a new device.

## Store pickup

1. Choose **Pick up in store** at checkout and select a store.
2. Wait for the "Ready for pickup" email or app notification. Most orders are ready within **2 hours** during store hours.
3. Bring a photo ID and your order number. The ID name must match the order, or the name of the person you added as an alternate pickup person at checkout.

Store pickup is free and available only for items marked "In stock at this store".

## Pickup points

You can also choose one of more than **1,200 partner pickup points** across the US, including lockers and partner shops. Pickup point delivery is free and takes **3-5 business days**, the same as Standard shipping. You will get a 6-digit pickup code by email and in the app.

Pickup points can't accept orders over **$1,500** or packages larger than 24 x 16 x 16 inches.

## How long we hold your order

Orders are held for **7 days** at stores and pickup points. If not collected in time, the order is cancelled and fully refunded.
