# Warranty Coverage

Every new product sold by ShopNest is covered by the **ShopNest 1-Year Limited Warranty**, in addition to any warranty from the manufacturer.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for **1 year from the delivery date**.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, such as cracked screens, drops, and liquid damage
- Normal wear and tear, such as scratches or worn-out cosmetic finishes
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Lost or stolen devices
- Battery capacity loss, unless capacity drops below **80%** within the warranty period
- Refurbished products, which have their own **90-day** warranty

## How to make a claim

1. Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order, and choose **Warranty claim**.
2. Describe the problem and upload photos or a short video if possible.
3. We reply within **2 business days**. If the claim is approved, we email you a free prepaid shipping label.
4. Back up and reset your device before sending it. We are not responsible for data on returned devices.

Once we receive the item, we repair it within **10 business days**. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model. If no replacement is available, we refund the original purchase price.

## ShopNest Care+

ShopNest Care+ extends your warranty and adds accidental damage protection (up to **2 claims per year**, with a $49 service fee per claim).

- Available for phones, laptops, and tablets
- 2-year or 3-year plans, priced at **10% or 15%** of the item price
- Must be purchased within **30 days of delivery**

You can add Care+ at checkout or later from **Account > Orders**.
