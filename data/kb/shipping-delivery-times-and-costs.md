# Delivery Times and Costs

ShopNest ships all US orders from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Orders placed before **2:00 PM Eastern Time** on a business day are processed and handed to the carrier the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET, or on weekends and US holidays, ship the next business day.

## Shipping options within the US

| Option | Delivery time after shipping | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $5.99, free on orders over $50 |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $14.99 |
| Next-Day | 1 business day | $24.99 |

- Next-Day is available to the 48 contiguous states only. It is not available to Alaska, Hawaii, or PO boxes.
- Standard shipping to Alaska and Hawaii takes 5-8 business days.
- The free shipping threshold applies to the order total after discounts and before tax.

## Carriers

We ship with UPS, FedEx, and USPS. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your address and shipping option. PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses are delivered by USPS with Standard shipping only.

## Signature required

Orders with a total over **$500** require an adult signature on delivery. If no one is home, the carrier will leave a notice and try again the next business day, up to 3 attempts in total.

## Changing your shipping option

You can upgrade your shipping option in **Account > Orders** until the order status changes to "Shipped". Once an order has shipped, the option and address can no longer be changed by ShopNest.
