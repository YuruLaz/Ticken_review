# International Shipping

ShopNest ships to **35 countries** outside the United States, including Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, all European Union countries, Japan, South Korea, and Australia. The full list of countries appears in the country menu at checkout. If your country is not listed, we cannot ship there yet.

## Delivery time and cost

- International orders are delivered in **7-14 business days** after shipping.
- Shipping is a flat **$29.99** per order.
- Shipping is free on international orders over **$300**.
- Express and Next-Day options are not available for international orders.

## Customs, duties, and taxes

Prices on ShopNest do not include import duties, customs fees, or local taxes (such as VAT or GST). These are charged by your country's customs office and are paid by you, usually to the carrier before or at delivery. If you refuse to pay these charges, the package is returned to us and we refund the item price minus the original shipping cost.

## Product restrictions

- Some brands do not allow export of their phones. If an item cannot be shipped to your country, it is marked "US only" on the product page.
- Spare lithium batteries and power banks over 100 Wh cannot be shipped internationally.
- Product warranty coverage abroad depends on the manufacturer. The ShopNest warranty still applies, but repairs are done at our US facility.

## Addresses

Enter your address in English (Latin characters) and include a phone number. Carriers use it to contact you about customs. International orders cannot be shipped to PO boxes.

## Returns from outside the US

You can return international orders within the same 30-day window as US orders, but you pay the return shipping cost, and duties already paid are not refunded by ShopNest.
