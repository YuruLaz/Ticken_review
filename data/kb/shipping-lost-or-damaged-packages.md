# Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is missing or arrives damaged, we will make it right with a replacement or a full refund, including shipping costs.

## My package arrived damaged

1. Report the damage within **48 hours of delivery**.
2. Take photos of the outer box, the shipping label, and the damaged item before throwing anything away.
3. Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order, and choose **Report a problem > Item arrived damaged**. Upload the photos.

We review damage reports within **1 business day**. Once approved, we send you a free return label and ship a replacement right away, or issue a full refund if you prefer or the item is out of stock. You do not have to wait for the damaged item to reach us before the replacement ships.

## Tracking says delivered, but I don't have it

Before contacting us, please:

- Wait until the end of the next business day. Carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered early.
- Check around your home, with neighbors, your building's mailroom, or your front desk.

If it still hasn't turned up, report it in **Report a problem > Package not received**.

## My package seems lost

A package is considered lost if tracking has not updated for **5 business days**, or it has not arrived **5 business days after the expected delivery date**.

## Deadline for reports

Missing or lost packages must be reported within **14 days** of the expected delivery date. After 14 days, we can no longer open an investigation with the carrier.

## What happens next

We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to **3 business days**. If the package is not found, we send a free replacement with Express shipping or issue a full refund, whichever you choose.
