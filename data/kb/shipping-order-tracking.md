# Tracking Your Order

You can follow your ShopNest order from the moment you place it until it reaches your door.

## Order statuses

- **Order placed:** We received your order and payment authorization.
- **Processing:** Your items are being picked and packed.
- **Shipped:** The package has been handed to the carrier.
- **Out for delivery:** The carrier expects to deliver it today.
- **Delivered:** The carrier marked the package as delivered.

## Finding your tracking number

When your order ships, we send a shipping confirmation email with the tracking number and carrier name. You can also find it at any time:

- **Website:** Account > Orders > select the order > Track package.
- **App:** Profile tab > Orders > select the order > Track.

If your order ships in multiple packages, each package has its own tracking number.

## Tracking without an account

Guest customers can track an order at shopnest.com/track by entering the order number and the email address used at checkout.

## When tracking updates

It can take up to **24 hours** after the shipping email for the carrier to show the first scan. During this time the tracking page may say "Label created" or "Pre-shipment". This is normal.

## Delivery alerts

Turn on push notifications in the ShopNest app to get alerts when your package ships, is out for delivery, and is delivered. You can also opt in to SMS delivery alerts in **Account > Notifications**.

## Tracking hasn't changed

If tracking shows no new scan for **5 business days**, please contact our support team with your order number and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
