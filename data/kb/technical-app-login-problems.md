# Can't Sign In to the App

If you can't sign in to the ShopNest app, work through the checks below.

## Check your details

- Make sure you are using the email address you signed up with. Email addresses are not case-sensitive, but passwords are.
- Check that Caps Lock is off and that your keyboard has not auto-corrected or auto-capitalized your password.
- If you created your account with **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, use the same button in the app. These accounts don't have a ShopNest password.

## "Account temporarily locked"

After **5 failed sign-in attempts**, the account is locked for **15 minutes** to protect it. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password using **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.

## Not receiving your 2FA code

- Make sure the time on your phone is set automatically. Authenticator codes fail if your device clock is wrong.
- For SMS codes, check that you have signal and wait up to 2 minutes before requesting a new code.
- If you have no access to your codes, use one of your backup codes.

## Signed in on the website but not in the app

- Update the app to the latest version. Old versions can fail to sign in.
- Check that the app has internet access. On iPhone, go to **Settings > ShopNest > Mobile Data**. On Android, check **Settings > Apps > ShopNest > Mobile data**.
- Turn off any VPN and try again.

## Apple or Google sign-in fails

Sign out of your Apple ID or Google account in your device settings and sign back in. Then try **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google** again.

## Signed out unexpectedly

App sessions last **90 days**. You are also signed out on all devices whenever your password is changed.
