# Common App Errors and How to Fix Them

If something goes wrong in the ShopNest app, you may see an error code. Here is what each code means and how to fix it.

## First steps for any error

1. Make sure you have the latest version of the app.
2. Close the app completely and open it again.
3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to rule out a network issue.
4. Restart your device.

## Error codes

- **SN-101 (No connection):** The app cannot reach our servers. Check your internet connection and turn off any VPN or ad blocker, then try again.
- **SN-203 (Session expired):** You have been signed out for security. Sessions in the app last **90 days**. Sign in again.
- **SN-310 (Cart could not be updated):** An item in your cart has changed in price or stock. Pull down to refresh your cart and review the items.
- **SN-404 (Item unavailable):** The product has been removed or is out of stock. Tap **Notify me** on the product page to get an alert when it is back.
- **SN-500 (Server error):** Something went wrong on our side. Wait 10 minutes and try again. Your cart is saved.

## The app crashes or freezes

- **Android:** Go to **Settings > Apps > ShopNest > Storage** and tap **Clear cache**. Do not tap "Clear data" unless you want to sign in again.
- **iPhone:** Delete the app and reinstall it from the App Store. Your account data is stored online and will not be lost.

## Still having problems

Contact support and tell us the error code, your device model, your operating system version, and the app version (found in **Profile > Settings > About**). Screenshots help us fix the problem faster.
