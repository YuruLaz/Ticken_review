# Installing the ShopNest App

The ShopNest app is free and available for iPhone, iPad, and Android devices. With the app you can shop, track orders, start returns, and chat with support.

## System requirements

- **iPhone and iPad:** iOS or iPadOS **16 or later**
- **Android:** Android **10 or later**, with Google Play services
- About **150 MB** of free storage space
- An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)

The app is not available for Windows, macOS, smartwatches, or Huawei devices without Google Play. On these devices, please use shopnest.com in your web browser.

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **ShopNest**. The publisher is listed as "ShopNest Inc."
3. Tap **Get**, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.

## Install on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Search for **ShopNest** and make sure the publisher is "ShopNest Inc."
3. Tap **Install**.

Only download the app from the App Store or Google Play. ShopNest never sends app installation files (APK files) by email or text message.

## After installing

Open the app and sign in with your existing ShopNest account, or create a new one. Your cart, wish list, and order history are synced between the app and the website.

## Updates

We release app updates about **every 2 weeks**. We support the current version and the previous **2 major versions**. Older versions will ask you to update before you can continue. Turn on automatic updates in your app store to always have the latest version.

## Installation fails

- Check that you have at least 150 MB of free space.
- Check that your device runs a supported operating system version.
- Restart your device and try again.
