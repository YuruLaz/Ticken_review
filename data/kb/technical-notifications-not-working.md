# Push Notifications Not Working

The ShopNest app can send push notifications about order shipping and delivery, price drops on wish list items, back-in-stock alerts, and replies from our support team. If you are not receiving them, follow these steps.

## 1. Check notifications in the app

Open the app and go to **Profile > Settings > Notifications**. Make sure the notification types you want are turned on. There are 4 types:

- Order updates
- Support messages
- Wish list price drops and back-in-stock alerts
- Deals and promotions

## 2. Check your device settings

- **iPhone:** Go to **Settings > Notifications > ShopNest** and turn on **Allow Notifications**. Also check that a Focus mode is not silencing the app.
- **Android:** Go to **Settings > Apps > ShopNest > Notifications** and turn on **Allow notifications**. Some phones also need the app excluded from **Battery optimization**, or notifications are delayed.

## 3. Make sure you are signed in

Notifications are linked to your account. If you are signed out, you will not receive order or support notifications. Signing in on a new device moves notifications to that device. You can receive notifications on up to **3 devices** at once.

## 4. Refresh the notification connection

Sign out of the app, close it completely, then open it and sign in again. This registers your device again.

## Things to know

- Notifications are only sent between **8:00 AM and 9:00 PM** in your local time zone, except for delivery and security alerts.
- Price drop alerts are sent when a wish list item drops by **10% or more**.
- Promotional notifications are limited to **2 per week**.

## Prefer email or SMS?

You can choose email or SMS alerts instead in **Account > Notifications** on the website.
