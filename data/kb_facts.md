# ShopNest KB facts (cheat sheet)

## Company-wide
- US online electronics store; prices in USD; warehouse in Columbus, Ohio
- Return window: 30 days from delivery (same everywhere)
- Order numbers: "SN-" + 8 digits (e.g. SN-20481937)
- Order/invoice records kept 7 years

## Billing
- Refunds go to original payment method; cancel before shipping = refund issued immediately
- Returns inspected within 2 business days of arrival; refund issued same day as approval
- Refund appears in 5-7 business days (PayPal 1-2 business days)
- Unopened items: full refund; opened phones/laptops: 15% restocking fee; opened accessories: no fee
- Original shipping refunded only if damaged, defective, or wrong item
- Payment: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, SplitPay
- SplitPay: 4 interest-free payments every 2 weeks, orders $100-$2,000, no credit score impact, $7 late fee if unpaid within 3 days
- Up to 5 saved cards; card authorized at order, captured at shipping; one charge per package
- Not accepted: cash on delivery, personal checks, wire transfers
- Invoice available within 24h of shipping; one per package; billing name/address editable once within 30 days
- Pending authorizations drop off in 3-5 business days; confirmed duplicates refunded within 1 business day
- Failed capture at shipping: order on hold 48h, then auto-cancelled
- 3 failed attempts in 24h: card paused for 24h

## Shipping
- Cutoff 2:00 PM ET on business days for same-day dispatch
- Standard 3-5 business days, $5.99, free over $50 (after discounts, before tax)
- Express 1-2 business days, $14.99; Next-Day 1 business day, $24.99 (contiguous 48 states only)
- Alaska/Hawaii Standard: 5-8 business days
- Carriers: UPS, FedEx, USPS; PO boxes/APO/FPO via USPS Standard only
- Orders over $500 need adult signature; 3 delivery attempts
- Shipping option/address changeable until shipped
- Tracking: first scan within 24h; guest tracking at shopnest.com/track; no scan for 5 business days = contact support
- International: 35 countries, 7-14 business days, flat $29.99, free over $300, no Express/Next-Day
- Duties/taxes paid by customer; refused package = refund minus original shipping
- No spare batteries/power banks over 100 Wh internationally; no PO boxes internationally
- International returns: 30 days, customer pays return shipping, duties not refunded
- Damage: report within 48h of delivery with photos; reviewed within 1 business day; replacement ships before damaged item returns
- Lost: no tracking update 5 business days, or 5 business days past expected delivery
- Report missing/lost within 14 days of expected delivery; carrier investigation up to 3 business days; replacement (Express) or full refund

## Account
- Reset link expires after 1 hour, single use, only latest link valid; max 5 reset emails/hour
- Password: min 10 chars, letter + number, not one of last 3; reset signs out other devices
- Reset email from no-reply@shopnest.com; Apple/Google sign-in accounts have no ShopNest password
- Email change: verification link valid 24h; old email notified; once per 7 days; one account per email
- Phone verified via 6-digit SMS code; up to 10 saved addresses
- Account deletion: blocked with undelivered orders/open returns/refunds; deactivated immediately, permanently deleted after 30 days (cancel by signing in); same email reusable after 30 days
- 2FA: authenticator app (codes every 30 s) or SMS (valid 10 min); 10 backup codes, single use; trusted device 30 days
- Lost 2FA recovery: up to 3 business days, identity verified via order history and payment method

## Technical
- App: iOS/iPadOS 16+, Android 10+ with Google Play services, ~150 MB free space
- No app for Windows, macOS, smartwatches, Huawei without Google Play
- Publisher "ShopNest Inc."; updates every ~2 weeks; current + previous 2 major versions supported
- Error codes: SN-101 no connection, SN-203 session expired, SN-310 cart could not be updated, SN-404 item unavailable, SN-500 server error (retry after 10 min)
- App sessions last 90 days; password change signs out all devices
- 5 failed sign-ins = 15-minute lock
- Notifications: 4 types; up to 3 devices; sent 8 AM-9 PM local (except delivery/security); price drop alert at 10%+; max 2 promo notifications/week
